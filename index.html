About Me

I'm from a small island in Sweden called Öland, also referred to as “the island of sun and winds.” It was peaceful growing up there, surrounded by nature and animals since I lived on a farm. However, there wasn't much to do, which led me to play video games a lot and eventually fall in love with them.

I love Indie games, and I have a soft spot for games that are experimental and unique. One of my favorite games of all times is Outer Wilds, which I think is a masterpiece in game design. It started my journey of seeking out game experiences that you can't find anywhere else.

Music has been a big part of my life, I've played the clarinet for +10 years and have even been part of the Royal Swedish Cadet Band.

On the Crown Princess's birthday, there is an annual celebration called Victoria Day, and I once participated as a background singer in one of the performances. Please don't look this up (￣▽￣*)
